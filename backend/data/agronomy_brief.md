## 1. Soybean - Yellow Mosaic Virus (YMV)
Vector: whitefly. Signs: yellow mosaic patches, stunting. Action: rogue infected plants,
control whitefly (thiamethoxam 25% WG @ 100 g/ha ONLY per label/SAU advice), yellow sticky traps.
Disclaimer: confirm with local agri officer before spraying.

## 2. Soybean - Girdle Beetle / Stem fly
Signs: ring cut on stem, wilting. Action: remove + destroy affected stems, early sowing,
pheromone monitoring. Insecticide only as per SAU label.

## 3. Wheat - Yellow Rust
Signs: yellow pustule stripes on leaves. Action: resistant varieties (HD-2967 etc.),
propiconazole 25% EC @ 0.1% ONLY if officer confirms; remove volunteer hosts.

## 4. Onion - Purple Blotch
Signs: purple-brown leaf lesions. Action: spacing + drainage, mancozeb 75% WP @ 2.5 g/L
per label; rotate crop.

## 5. Cotton - Pink Bollworm
Signs: rosette flowers, bored bolls. Action: pheromone traps 8/ha, destroy crop residue,
neem + recommended dose only.

## Safety (read aloud in voice + show on card)
- Always read label, wear PPE, keep away from children, never mix without guidance.
- Dosage here is indicative - follow State Agri University / dealer + officer confirmation.
- If >30% plants affected, call Kisan Call Centre 1800-180-1551.
